Why tint your windows

What quality film actually does for your ride.

Good tint is more than a look. In Florida sun it changes how your car feels the moment you get in — cooler seats, softer light, and glass you're not squinting through.

Beat the Florida heat

Ceramic film rejects a big share of the sun's heat, so your cabin cools down faster and stays that way — even after hours in a parking lot.

Block up to 99% UV

Quality film filters out the UV rays that burn skin, crack dashboards and fade upholstery — protecting you and your interior on every drive.

Real privacy & security

Darker glass keeps eyes off your passengers and whatever's in the car. Less on display means less to worry about wherever you park.

Kill the glare

Cut the harsh glare off the hood, the water and oncoming headlights. Easier eyes, calmer drives and a clearer view of the road.

Protect your interior

Dashes, seats and trim take a beating from constant sun. Film slows the fading and cracking so your interior lasts and holds its value.

A cleaner, sharper look

Nothing finishes a vehicle like a clean, even tint. Richie matches the shade to your car so it looks factory-sharp from every angle.

The install

A real mobile studio — clean, precise, bubble-free.

Richie runs a proper mobile shop: computer-cut film, professional plotter and a controlled setup on board — so every piece is cut to fit your exact glass before it ever touches the window.

That means tight edges, no razor marks on your seals and a finish that lays down clean. The same care goes into cars, trucks and SUVs alike — and into repairing or replacing film that's gone bad.

  • Computer-cut film matched to your exact windows
  • Clean, even lines and a smooth, bubble-free finish
